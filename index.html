How to play

StrikePress a direction and you strike that way. QWE / AD / ZXC are the eight directions. Arrow keys work for the four sides. TouchTap or swipe toward the side an enemy is coming from. GuardsGlow blue — a single strike bounces off. Hit them twice, fast. SumoHuge. When it flashes red it is about to charge — strike it right then to counter, or you lose two hearts. Power-upsClear a wave and pick one of three cards: nunchucks (longer reach), a heart, swifter strikes and more. PauseEsc. Mute with M.
